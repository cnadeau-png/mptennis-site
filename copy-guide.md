# MPTennis Copy Guide

## The "we" rule

This is the rule for every line of copy on the MPTennis site, app, emails, and ads.

### Cade = the credential
Any sentence about competing, ranking, UTR, on-court skill, or tennis authority **uses Cade's name**. That's his to own. He's the face of the brand.

✅ "Cade is an 11.20 UTR competing player."
✅ "Coached by Cade — NCAA captain, top-40 Canada."
❌ "We're an 11.20 UTR competing player." (nonsense — credentials are individual)

### We = the system and mission
"We" refers to MPTennis as a brand and to Cade + Chris as business partners. Use it for:
- The system, methodology, philosophy
- The mission and audience
- Programs and offerings

✅ "We build better players."
✅ "We work with competitive juniors building toward college."
✅ "We built MPTennis to put pro-level coaching in reach of club players."

### Never "we" in the hero headline
Keep the first impression on Cade and his credentials. "We" earns trust **lower** on the page, after the authority is already established.

### Junior copy always uses "we"
Parents are evaluating a program, not a person. "We" signals structure, more than one coach, a real organization.

✅ "We work with competitive juniors building toward college."
✅ "Our junior program builds players from rating 3.0 through college-level."
❌ "Cade works with juniors..." (sounds like a one-man freelance gig)

### Chris is behind the scenes
Chris is Cade's father, co-founder, and business partner. He coaches Cade off-camera and runs the business side. Chris is **not** the face of the brand. Don't put him in hero copy or video thumbnails. He appears in:
- The About section (as the team behind Cade)
- "We" statements about the company
- Off-camera training references

### Audience naming
- **"Club Players"** — adult recreational/competitive (3.0–5.0 NTRP). Don't say "amateur" — sounds dismissive.
- **"Juniors"** or **"Competitive Juniors"** — junior players, especially those targeting college.
- **"Junior parents"** — only when speaking specifically to the buyer for junior coaching.

Never call players "students." They're players.

### Tone
- **Direct, not aspirational.** "Get better" beats "unlock your potential."
- **Specific over vague.** "11.20 UTR" beats "highly ranked."
- **Confident, not cocky.** State credentials plainly. Don't oversell.
- **No hype words.** Avoid: "unlock," "transform," "dominate," "crush," "elite" (overused).

### Examples — same idea, three layers

**Hero (Cade's name + credential):**
> Cade Nadeau · 11.20 UTR · NCAA Captain

**Mid-page (we-statement, mission):**
> We built MPTennis to put pro-level coaching in reach of club players and juniors — anywhere in the world.

**Junior section (we-statement, program):**
> We work with competitive juniors building toward college tennis.
