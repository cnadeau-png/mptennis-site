Best Hybrid Strings for Club Players? I Tested 3 Setups

By Cade Nadeau · 4 min read

The short answer: the best overall hybrid for most players is Zero in the mains with Sync in the crosses. For the most control, go Vivo + Sync. For the most comfort and pocketing, go Slap + Sync. I strung all three at 52 lbs.

The test

I strung three Restring hybrid setups at the same tension, 52 lbs. Sync, their most controlled string, goes in the crosses in all three. If you've played ALU Power, Sync is my best comparison, and I actually think it's better.

Setup (main + cross)Best forFeel
Vivo + SyncMost controlCrispy, firm and the stiffest of the three
Zero + SyncBest overallRight down the middle, the best of both worlds
Slap + SyncMost comfortBest pocketing. Slap feels like a softer RPM Blast

Vivo + Sync: most control

If you want the most controlled hybrid and don't want full Sync, this is it. It comes in and out of the string bed quickly. If you have elbow, wrist or shoulder issues, this is the stiffest of the three, so pick another one. It's a good match for a softer frame. One small thing: it sometimes sailed a bit long for me.

Zero + Sync: best overall

This is the setup I think works best for most people. I strung it in my Defyer 100 and it was unbelievable. I worried a 100 would spray balls long, and it didn't. Tension maintenance and playability are very good.

Slap + Sync: most comfortable

The best pocketing of the three. The ball sits on the strings longer. I really like it in the Boom Pro. If you have a stiff frame, this is a good way to save your arm and still get Sync in the cross.

How to pick

  • Stiff frame or arm issues: Slap + Sync.
  • Soft frame and you want control: Vivo + Sync.
  • Not sure: Zero + Sync.

None of the three lacked pace, spin or snapback.

FAQ

What is the best hybrid string setup for club players?

Cade's best overall pick is Restring Zero in the mains with Sync in the crosses. It works for most players.

What is the most comfortable hybrid?

Of the three tested, Slap + Sync. It has the best pocketing and plays softer than Vivo.

What tension should I string a hybrid at?

Cade strung all three at 52 lbs for the test. Adjust a few pounds up or down to fit your racquet and swing.
