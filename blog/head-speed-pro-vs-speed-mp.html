Head Speed Pro vs Speed MP: Which Is Actually Better?

By Cade Nadeau · 5 min read

The short answer: both are 100 square inch frames, but they play differently. The Speed MP (16x19) gives you more spin, a higher launch and lots of free power. The Speed Pro (18x20) has a lower launch, better depth control, and much better touch and feel. I liked the Pro more.

Specs

These are the Auxetic 2.0 versions. Specs as listed by Tennis Warehouse:

Speed MPSpeed Pro
Head size100 sq in100 sq in
String pattern16x1918x20
Strung weight11.1 oz11.6 oz
Swingweight330333
Power levelLow to mediumLow

Beam width and stiffness are the same. The big difference is the string pattern, and a little weight.

Speed MP: spin and free power

Compared to my Radical, the MP gives a lot more pop. It spins and the ball moves through the air, but it's not hitting the back fence like some 100s can. Serves get lots of free power and spin.

I added a couple of grams of lead at 3 and 9 because it was a touch unstable on volleys. The downside for me: touch and feel. It's a bit muted, and sometimes I didn't feel connected to the ball.

Speed Pro: control and feel

From the back, the Pro has a slightly lower launch and a little less spin, because of the 18x20 bed. But my access to depth was much better. I didn't need any customization. It was a beast stock.

Where the Pro blows the MP away is touch and feel. I felt more connected on every shot, especially slices. At the net it feels like a board. It just won't budge.

Which one should you get?

  • Speed MP if you want spin, launch and free power on serve.
  • Speed Pro if you want control, depth and feel, and you like a solid racquet at the net.

I liked the Pro more. But test both on your own shots before you buy.

FAQ

What is the difference between the Head Speed Pro and Speed MP?

Both are 100 square inch frames. The MP has a 16x19 pattern for more spin and launch. The Pro has an 18x20 pattern, a bit more weight, more control and better feel.

Is the Head Speed Pro or Speed MP better?

Cade liked the Speed Pro more for its touch, feel and depth control. The MP is better if you want spin and free power.

Does the Head Speed MP need lead tape?

Cade added a couple of grams at 3 and 9 for more stability on volleys. The Pro felt stable stock.
