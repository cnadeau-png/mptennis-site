The 5 Most Used Strings in College Tennis (From a College Player)

By Cade Nadeau · 4 min read

The short answer: from what I saw playing college tennis, the most used strings were: 1. Solinco Hyper-G, 2. Solinco Tour Bite, 3. Babolat RPM Blast, 4. Luxilon ALU Power, and tied 5th, Solinco Confidential and Volkl Cyclone. College men almost all use polyester. That doesn't mean you should copy them.

This is my opinion, from what I saw in college tennis, mostly on the men's side. All of these are polyester strings. Some women's players use hybrids.

The list

#StringMy take
1Solinco Hyper-GEverywhere, men and women. I think it's overrated because it loses tension, but a lot of people love it.
2Solinco Tour BiteBites the ball well, like a better version of Cyclone. Holds tension better.
3Babolat RPM BlastThe go to for players with Babolat frames. Very popular with guys and girls.
4Luxilon ALU PowerA classic. You know what you're getting. Not the most arm friendly, and very expensive.
5 (tie)Solinco ConfidentialMy string of choice for years. Surprisingly rare in college racquets.
5 (tie)Volkl CycloneSolid and bites the ball well, but I found it lost a lot of tension.

Honorable mention: Head Lynx Tour, a solid string I used when I first got my Extreme Tours.

What club players should take from this

  • College players swing fast and break strings often. A stiff full poly fits that.
  • If you play a few times a week, a stiff poly can lose its playability before it breaks, and it can be hard on your arm.
  • Pick the string for your game and your arm, not the one the best players use. Find your fit, then stop shopping.

FAQ

What strings do college tennis players use?

Mostly polyester. The most common Cade saw were Solinco Hyper-G, Solinco Tour Bite, Babolat RPM Blast, Luxilon ALU Power, Solinco Confidential and Volkl Cyclone.

What is the most popular string in college tennis?

In Cade's experience, Solinco Hyper-G.

Should club players use the same strings as college players?

Not necessarily. Stiff polyesters suit fast swings and frequent restringing. Many club players do better with a softer setup or a hybrid.
