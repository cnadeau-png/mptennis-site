Wilson Defyer 100 Review: I Like It More Than the 98, Except for One Thing

By Cade Nadeau · 4 min read

The short answer: after a few weeks of testing, I like the Wilson Defyer 100 more than the 98. It's not a typical 100. It has less power than other 100s, more control, and great feel. The one thing holding it back is stability in stock form, mostly on returns. It's still in my top five frames of the year.

Not your typical 100

You've got EZONE 100s, VCORE 100s, Pure Aeros and Pure Drives. Those frames give you the power you expect from a 100 square inch racquet. The Defyer 100 isn't like that. It didn't give me nearly as much.

For me, that's a good thing. Most 100s give me too much, and balls fly long. With this one you get what you want from a 100, like the bigger sweet spot and a thicker beam with enough power, plus a good amount of control.

Feel and maneuverability

In hand it feels a lot like the Defyer 98, but lighter. It swings through the air really easily. The maneuverability is better than your typical 100.

I actually like it more than the 98 at the net and on touch shots, when I'm not getting balls that push me around. I struggled with volleys on the 98. With the 100, I feel like I can put a ball on a dime, which makes it great for coaching.

The one flaw: stability

In stock form, the stability for advanced play is lacking. On groundstrokes, when the ball comes in heavier, the hoop gives out a little. I felt it most on the return, and a little at the net. It felt mushy and weak, both in the centre and off centre.

The hoop doesn't have enough mass to hold up against really heavy spin that pushes you back, or balls that are flat and hard. I'd rather defend with the 98, because it holds up more.

Control and strings

Control wasn't an issue at all. I normally play Sync and Sync at 50 to 55 lbs. For this test I strung it at 52 with a softer poly in the mains, and it was very controlled.

Can lead fix it?

I added some weight to try to fix the stability. The issues didn't go away fully. But I think lead is the number one play here, to bring up the swingweight and balance. If it fixes that one thing, the racquet goes from great to excellent. Maybe this is a racquet I could switch to. There you go, I said it.

Defyer 100 or Defyer 98?

Try both. The 100 isn't a typical 100, and the 98 isn't a typical 98 either.

FAQ

Is the Wilson Defyer 100 powerful?

Not like other 100s such as the EZONE 100, VCORE 100, Pure Aero or Pure Drive. You get the bigger sweet spot and enough power, with more control.

What is the Defyer 100's weakness?

Stability in stock form for advanced play. Against heavy or hard, flat balls the hoop can give out, mostly on the return and a bit at the net. Adding lead is the fix to try.

Should I get the Defyer 100 or the 98?

Try both. Go 100 for maneuverability if stability doesn't worry you. Go 98 if you prefer a smaller head or more stability on defence.
