Why swingweight

Two racquets at the same weight can feel completely different.

Static weight is what your racquet weighs sitting on a scale. Swingweight is what it weighs through the air. The difference can make the same frame feel like a wand or a sledgehammer — and it's the single best predictor of whether a racquet will help your game or hurt it.
